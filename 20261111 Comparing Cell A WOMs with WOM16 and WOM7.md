$$l_{WLS}\approx 30\mu\text{m}$$
#### Thickness compared to uncoated WOM
$$d_{WLS}= -\frac12\text{log}(\frac{\Phi_T}{\Phi_{uncoated}})\cdot l_{WLS}$$
