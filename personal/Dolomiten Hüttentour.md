**Section 1:** On day 1 the journey begins with a car/bus ride to the start points of Sillian or Kartitsch. After 3 – 4 days hiking you’ll reach the hut named “Valentinalm”, which represents the end of the first section. From here it is possible to continue directly onto the second section of the KHW to Thörl-Maglern, over Mauthner Alm or take the Romans path into the valley.
