# Forward Physics Facility
- **Location:** CERN LHC
- **Purpose:** Detect forward-produced neutrinos and new physics phenomena
- **Detector:** FASERν2, a large-volume detector with a spectrometer, calorimeters, and muon detector
- **Highlights:** Aims to explore physics beyond the Standard Model in the forward region of the LHC
