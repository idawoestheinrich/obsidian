- <mark class="hltr-yellow">Selections on μ- beam data are made to 1) exclude hadron (π-)  contamination of the beam, 2) filter any rare high-energy processes of  μ-’s that may be occurring, and 3) filter any electronic noise that may  be present.</mark> [Page 30](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BW8W8JZ5?page=30&annotation=CWP77BKH)

###### Summary [Page 67](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BW8W8JZ5?page=67&annotation=7NI72X67)
- <mark class="hltr-yellow">Performance studies have continued to be done on the Tile Calorimeter Test Beam to  validate the performance of Upgrade electronics, with comparisons to Geant4 Monte-Carlo.</mark> [Page 68](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BW8W8JZ5?page=68&annotation=553IM6UW)
- <mark class="hltr-yellow">dE/dx distributions give similar spectra for each beam momentum, with some known  physical effects affecting the distribution width.</mark> [Page 68](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BW8W8JZ5?page=68&annotation=PTYCDEIN)
- <mark class="hltr-yellow">When comparing the two Upgrade readouts, ROD and FELIX, dE/dx distributions show  similar responses when compared to Monte-Carlo and to one another; the qualitative agreement with Monte-Carlo and with one another improves as beam momentum  increases.</mark> [Page 68](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BW8W8JZ5?page=68&annotation=36SU6A3T)
- <mark class="hltr-yellow">In addition, it is seen that quantitative response of the 97.5% truncated means is nearly  identical even in lower momentum ranges when comparing the ROD and FELIX readout  systems.</mark> [Page 68](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BW8W8JZ5?page=68&annotation=MFPLIKKW)
- <mark class="hltr-yellow">By using 97.5% truncated means, it is seen that both the BC- and D-layers show good  agreement with Monte-Carlo (2-4% for either layer), with the D-layer having the best  overall across all beam momenta.</mark> [Page 68](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BW8W8JZ5?page=68&annotation=HUHCUWLF)
- <mark class="hltr-yellow">Generally, both layers perform best (more in line with Monte-Carlo) at lower beam  momenta, and average response per layer increases with increasing beam momentum.</mark> [Page 68](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BW8W8JZ5?page=68&annotation=TGDFF8JC)
- <mark class="hltr-yellow">ROC curves and energy deposition plots show excellent signal-background separation.</mark> [Page 68](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BW8W8JZ5?page=68&annotation=PFX5V9HP)
- <mark class="hltr-yellow">Projective μ-’s in the TileCal Test Beam setup show consistent and reliable results using the  Phase-II Upgrade electronics, readout configurations, and μ- trigger schemes and is well on its way to being ready for integration into the ATLAS TileCal for HL-LHC!</mark> [Page 68](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/BW8W8JZ5?page=68&annotation=HDM5YHNE)
