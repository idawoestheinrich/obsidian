### What does “perovskite” mean?
- **Perovskite** refers to a specific **crystal structure**, typically written as **ABX₃*
- Common perovskites used in scintillators include **lead halide perovskites**, such as:
    - CsPbBr₃    
    - CsPbCl₃
    - Hybrid organic–inorganic perovskite
### How do perovskite scintillators work?
1. Incoming radiation (X-rays, γ-rays) deposits energy in the material
2. The energy excites electrons in the crystal
3. When the electrons relax back to lower energy states, the material emits **visible photons**    
4. These photons are detected by photodetectors (PMTs, SiPMs, cameras, etc.)
