###### Summary for veto detectors
- <mark class="hltr-yellow">For UBT / BT plane:  - Combinatorial muon pairs have always one muon passing through BT plane max ~1.8x1.8x7m: tagging that muon is enough to reject full background. Single or multi-plane to be defined, after SBT is fixed. - Rates and efficiency requirements defined for several muon shield design: max 150–750k/cm2 in central region in Y, sides in X, one muon every 10–60 ns. - Need extrapolation of tracks from SST (multiple scattering significant), only valid for non-bouncing muons. - Muon DIS in He from front muons can be vetoed by incoming muon passing through UBT: need more statistics to evaluate veto power needed for this, potentially more stringent than requirement from combinatorics.  - For SBT:  - (very important) redundancy for combinatorial background, and for identifying side (bouncing) muons. - Required for tagging the dominant neutrino DIS source: from He. - Required for tagging muon DIS in He from bouncing</mark> [Page 16](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JSLQR5SM?page=16&annotation=J9588V9B)
- <mark class="hltr-yellow">Need to study dedicated GNN for each DIS source.</mark> [Page 16](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JSLQR5SM?page=16&annotation=QWMLEMIR)
