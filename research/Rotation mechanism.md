We use a stepper motor controlled by a Driver on a [[Raspberry Pi]] to rotate the PCBs around the wall of the WOM. 
The stepper Motor will be mounted on one of two horizontal platforms that are attached to the linear moving system. The two platforms are needed to stabilise the gear hub (rotating axis). For that purpose, a ball bearing with an inner ø=1cm is pressed into each platform. 
On the hub is a reely polyacetal bevel gear module type that translates horizontal to vertical rotation.
