### Wavelength-Shifting Optical Modules (WOMs)

The light guides and collectors used for the [[SBT]] detector are [[WOM]]s. The concept was first introduced for the IceCube large-volume extension in a next-generation experiment called IceCube-Gen2 [[Schulte2013ALS]], further described in [[bastianquerner2022WOM]].

The detection principle of the [[SHiP]] [[WOM]] is illustrated in the figure below.

![Schematic illustration of the working principle of a WOM tube.](THEO_WOM_Working_Prinziple.png)  
*Figure 1: Schematic illustration of the working principle of a [[WOM]] tube. The scintillation photon $\gamma$ coming from the left passes through the [[PMMA]] vessel and hits the surface of the coated tube, where the [[Wavelength-shifting molecules|WLS]] paint absorbs it. The [[Wavelength-shifting molecules|WLS]]  paint re-emits a lower-energy photon $\gamma'$, captured with an efficiency of $74.1\%$, due to TIR. Taken from [[AnnikaHollnagel]].*

It consists of a [[PMMA]] tube ($20\,\si{cm}$ high, outer diameter $6\,\si{cm}$, wall thickness $2\,\si{mm}$ or $3\,\si{mm}$), coated on the inner and outer surface with a [[Wavelength-shifting molecules|WLS]] dye. [[UV]] photons $\gamma$ coming from the liquid scintillator are absorbed in the [[WLS]] paint layer. The [[Wavelength-shifting molecules|WLS]] paint reemits secondary photons $\gamma'$ in the visible spectrum. These photons are then guided toward the ends of the tube via [[Total internal reflection|TIR]].

In the liquid scintillator cells of the [[Surrounding background tagger|SBT]] detector, the [[WOM]]s are placed in transparent [[PMMA]] vessels to protect the [[Wavelength-shifting molecules|WLS]] dye from the liquid scintillator and to increase the [[Total internal reflection|TIR]] due to the lower index of refraction of air ($n_\text{air} \approx 1$) compared to that of the [[PMMA]] ($n_\text{PMMA}\approx 1.50$). The [[PMMA]] vessel also allows the liquid scintillator to be on the inside of the [[WOM]]. Since the typical emission of the fluid scintillator used in the [[SHiP]] [[Surrounding background tagger|SBT]] detector occurs below $450\,\si{nm}$ with a peak at $\approx 365\,\si{nm}$, UV transparent [[PMMA]] is chosen for the material of the [[WOM]] and its vessel.
