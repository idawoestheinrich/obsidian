### **NuTeV (Neutrinos at the Tevatron)**

- **Location:** Fermilab, Illinois
- **Purpose:** Study deep inelastic neutrino scattering to probe nucleon structure
- **Detector:** 700-ton detector with alternating steel and particle detectors
- **Highlights:** Provided precise measurements of neutrino interactions, contributing to the understanding of the Standard Model
