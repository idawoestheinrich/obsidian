# Flavor mixing
- Flavor eigenstates produced in weak interaction
	- Propagation eigenstates are mass eigenstates in vacuum, but not in matter - if we are in infinite dense matter are the propagation eigenstates the flavor eigenstates 
	- In very dense matter the potential can be 6 orders of magnitude higher
	- Scale that sets everything fermi constant times the neutrinos mass
- Propagation eigenstates 
	- Have different energies - different phases
- Propagation in a medium - "feel the energy of the medium/potential", known from photons
- Electrons produce a weak  potential
	- Impact on electron neutrinos  - not $\nu_\mu, \nu_\tau$
	- Why can muons not be produced, 30MeV temperature - average energy 3x20MeV, but not quite the muon energy 
- Self-induced neutrino refraction 
	- neutrinos also produce a weak potential 
	- They can be in superposition
	- triggers the neutrino to not be in the flavor eigenstates
	- Non-linear effect
- Where is this relevant? 
	- Core-Collapse Supernovae
	- Iron core without fusion 
	- Core collapses up to nuclear densities
	- even neutrinos get to thermal equilibrium with the surrounding matter - are trapped
- Rebounce - launches shock waves 
- Shock wave stalls, revived by neutrinos depositing energy 
	- Neutrinos power the explosion - but it could also explode without neutrinos - we can not measure the flavor of the emitted neutrinos anyway as the supernova is so far away 
	- Depending on flavor 
- Flavor conversions
	- We can not have direct flavor mixing because of the huge matter potential 
	- BUT the neutrino potential leads to flavor conversion - non linear effect
