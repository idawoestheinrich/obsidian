### DUNE (Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment)

- **Location:** Near Fermilab, Illinois (near detector); Sanford Underground Research Facility, South Dakota (far detector)
    
- **Purpose:** Investigate neutrino oscillations, CP violation in the lepton sector, and proton decay
    
- **Detector:** Four modules of liquid argon time projection chambers (LArTPCs)
    
- **Highlights:** Designed to study neutrino interactions over an 800 km baseline, DUNE aims to address fundamental questions about the universe's matter-antimatter imbalance
