### Optical coupling
- in contrast to - total internal reflection requirement, coupling between scintillator and the PM must allow maximum light transmission 
- air between the PM and the scintillator results in trapping portions of the light in the scintillator material. 
- optical contact with a material with a refractive index as close as possible to the scintillator and PM window, for example, silicon grease or oil

has to have a similar refractive index as the objects that need to be coupled
Should it be in the theory part? - Probably not
