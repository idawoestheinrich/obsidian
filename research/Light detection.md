### Light Detection

In the [[CheapCal]] prototype, the [[WLS]] fibres collect photons and direct them to [[SiPM]] at both ends of each fibre, where a voltage signal proportional to the number of photons can be measured.  
The working principle of [[SiPM]] will be discussed.
In the [[Surrounding background tagger|SBT]], the same kind of [[SiPM]] will be used for the readout of the [[WOM]], but due to the lower complexity of the readout, a [[PMT]] will be used for the readout in the quality control setup. The [[PMT]] used for this setup is the Hamamatsu [[PMT]] R877-10 [[2016PMTsensitivity]]. The next section will discuss the working principle of [[PMT]] in general and the specific characteristics of R877.
