## Theory on HNLs
- No right handed neutrino in SM
	- No dirac mass term
- Majorana mass term - violate lepton number 
- BUT neutrino oscilaltions, very small masses
- Sticking to Majorana 
### Tree level Seesaw mechanism
- Type I, II, III See-saw
	- HNL, scalar Higgs triplet, fermionic tripplet
Type 1 - add sterile "right handed" neutrinos
