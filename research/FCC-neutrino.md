### **FCCν (Future Circular Collider Neutrino)**

- **Location:** CERN
- **Purpose:** Investigate high-energy neutrino interactions and potential new physics
- **Detector:** Part of the FCC-hh (hadron-hadron) collider program
- **Highlights:** Aims to achieve indirect sensitivity to new physics up to a scale of 70 TeV
