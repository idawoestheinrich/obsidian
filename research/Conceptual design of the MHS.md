###### Conclusions
- <mark class="hltr-yellow">Conceptual magnetic design of the MHS has advanced:  • Cross-section optimized to compress the field in the iron core to the target value of 1.9 T;  • Remanent field studies revealed that a g/lm ratio of 2 ‰ would imply that residual field (Bres) does not strongly depend on material properties (Hc) and equivalent load for the crane is below 20 tons;  • Target field reached in the core with excellent homogeneity;  • Coil design depends on the choice of the power converters. Preliminary design of the 250/500 A coils available;  • To be discussed how to handle the coil ends due to space constraints.</mark> [Page 20](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/K6J5Y9PH?page=20&annotation=BMPKWKCQ)
