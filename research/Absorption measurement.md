The absorption measurement would be interesting because it directly tells us something about the quality of the (wavelength shifting) [[WLS Color coating]] of the [[WOM]]s - so about the density of [[bis-MSB]] molecules.


The WLS color absorps photons with a wavelength between 300 and 400nm with a very high efficiency and emitts photons with a peak at 420 nm. 
To avoid detecting the emitted light, a [[LED]] with 365 nm is used and a [[Photosensor]] sensitive up to a wavelength of 370nm.
