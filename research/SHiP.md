# Search for Hidden Particles
- **Location:** CERN SPS (Super Proton Synchrotron)
- **Purpose:** Explore hidden sector physics, including dark photons and heavy neutrinos
- **Detector:** Beam dump experiment with a dedicated detector system for measuring decays of feebly interacting particles
- **Highlights:** Utilizes a 400 GeV proton beam to search for new physics beyond the Standard Model
