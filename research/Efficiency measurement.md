The efficiency measurement is important because it tells something about the photon transport efficiency of the whole [[WOM]] and its [[WLS Color coating]] via [[Total internal reflection]]. 
In the Efficiency measurement, non-local characteristics of the [[WLS Color coating]] will affect the measurement, like
- Photon transport efficiency 
- Purity of the entire surface

It is measured with a [[WOM]] standing on a [[PMT]] and lighted by a pulsed [[LED]]. 
[[Optical Coupling]] between the [[WOM]] and [[PMT]] is needed to lose as few photons as possible. 
Calculating the absolute efficiency of the [[WOM]] might be hard, according to Bastian (QC WOMs for IceCube), because many things can influence the measurement:
- Magnetic fields in the lab - need to be measured 
- Myons - they have a Myon shield for the ice cube setup
- Angle of the photons reaching the [[PMT]]
It also might not be important, because we measure with a [[PMT]] and in the [[SBT]] we will measure with [[SiPM]]s
