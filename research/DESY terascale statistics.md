The next edition of the DESY Terascale Statistics School will provide an introduction to key statistical methods used in particle-physics data analysis. The school is aimed primarily at PhD students working on LHC or other HEP experiments, but is also open to interested Master’s students and post-doctoral researchers.

**Core topics:**

-  **Basics**
-  **Hypothesis testing**
-  **Parameter estimation**
-  **Confidence intervals**
-  **Systematic uncertainties**
-  **Machine learning**
-  **Unfolding**

- **Roger Barlow**
- **Glen Cowan**
- **Stephen Jiggins**
- **Lydia Brenner**
