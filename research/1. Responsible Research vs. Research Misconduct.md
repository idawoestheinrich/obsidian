What is research misconduct? 
- No standard universal definition
- Core concepts:
	- [ALLEA](https://allea.org/portfolio-item/european-code-of-conduct-2023/) - All European Academics code of conduct 
	- [DFG guidlines](https://zenodo.org/records/14281892) 
	- Institutional policies ([HU](https://www.hu-berlin.de/en/research/good-academic-practice/hu-statute-gwp_en.pdf))
	- Main forms of misbehavior:
		- **Fabrication** 
		- **Falsification**
		- **Plagiarism**
### Fabrication and Falsification
- **Fabrication** 
	- Are false statement - entirely makeing up data for studies that never took place
- **Falsification**
	- manipulating data from existing datasets 
- Sabotaging the research of others 
- or being involved in the misconduct  of others
- research can sometimes lead to wrong assumtions
- Be gracefull with errors of others
