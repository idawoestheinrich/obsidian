### **MuCol 10 TeV**

- **Concept:** Design study for a muon collider operating at 10 TeV center-of-mass energy
    
- **Objective:** Develop a detector optimized for high-energy muon collisions
    
- **Features:** Includes a calorimeter barrel with updated geometry for enhanced energy resolution
