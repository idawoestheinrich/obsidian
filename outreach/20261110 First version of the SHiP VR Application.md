### How to enter SHiP on your phone
- Download VRChat from App or Playstore
- Create an account or log in 
- Enter any world (f.e. ATLAS Demo or ATLAS Experiment at CERN)
- Once you are in the world, tap the menu (three lines on the lower right corner)
- On the horizontal phone tap settings (a little gear item)
- On the upper right corner tap full screen to see all the settings
- Search for "Comfort & Safety" or something similar
- Under Safety find "Show Community Labs" and activate it 
- Then Klick on worlds and search for "SHiP Test" (for me it is the 3rd option)
- Klick SHiP Test and join 
- Have fun! 😁
