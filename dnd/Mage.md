### Actions

Multiattack. The mage makes three Arcane Burst attacks.

Arcane Burst. _Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:_ +6, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. _Hit:_ 16 (3d8 + 3) Force damage.

Spellcasting. The mage casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):

- At will: [Detect Magic](https://5e.tools/spells.html#detect%20magic_xphb), [Light](https://5e.tools/spells.html#light_xphb), [Mage Armor](https://5e.tools/spells.html#mage%20armor_xphb) (included in AC), [Mage Hand](https://5e.tools/spells.html#mage%20hand_xphb), [Prestidigitation](https://5e.tools/spells.html#prestidigitation_xphb)
    
- 2/day each: [Fireball](https://5e.tools/spells.html#fireball_xphb) (level 4 version), [Invisibility](https://5e.tools/spells.html#invisibility_xphb)
    
- 1/day each: [Cone of Cold](https://5e.tools/spells.html#cone%20of%20cold_xphb), [Fly](https://5e.tools/spells.html#fly_xphb)
    

### Bonus Actions

Misty Step (3/Day). The mage casts [Misty Step](https://5e.tools/spells.html#misty%20step_xphb), using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

### Reactions

Protective Magic (3/Day). The mage casts [Counterspell](https://5e.tools/spells.html#counterspell_xphb) or [Shield](https://5e.tools/spells.html#shield_xphb) in response to the spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
