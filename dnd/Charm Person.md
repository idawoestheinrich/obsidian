| One Humanoid you can see within range makes a Wisdom saving throw. It does so with [Advantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#advantage_xphb) if you or your allies are fighting it. On a failed save, the target has the [Charmed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#charmed_xphb) condition until the spell ends or until you or your allies damage it. The [Charmed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#charmed_xphb) creature is [Friendly](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#friendly%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) to you.
